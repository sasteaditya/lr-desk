What this covers

This policy explains how Aditya Transport uses information in its internal LR Desk system and in the email and WhatsApp messages it sends about vehicle placements, lorry receipts (LRs) and deliveries.

Information we use

  • Business details of consignors, consignees and billed parties: name, address, GSTIN, email address, mobile number and delivery location.
  • Shipment details: vehicle number, driver name and mobile, invoice and material details, LR and placement numbers, and delivery status.
  • Messages you send to our WhatsApp number in reply to our messages.

How we use it

  • To place vehicles, issue LRs, share LR copies, and inform you about loading, transit and delivery.
  • To send drivers the loading and delivery address and location.
  • To answer your queries and keep records required for our business and tax purposes.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with the service providers we use to run this system and send messages: Google (data storage and email) and Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business messaging). They process the information on our behalf under their own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep shipment records for as long as needed for business, accounting and legal requirements.

Your choices

If you do not want to receive WhatsApp or email updates, or want your details corrected or removed, write to info@adityatransport.com or reply STOP on WhatsApp.
